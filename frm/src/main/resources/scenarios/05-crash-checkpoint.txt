## Crash setelah checkpoint selesai: recovery dimulai dari checkpoint terakhir (FR-06, FR-07)
table acc
seed acc 0:1 1 100
seed acc 0:2 2 200

begin T1
update T1 acc 0:1 1 150
commit T1
checkpoint
expect-result OK

begin T2
update T2 acc 0:2 2 250
commit T2
begin T3
update T3 acc 0:1 1 999
insert T3 acc 0:3 3 300
flush-table acc

crash
# T1 sudah aman di disk sebelum checkpoint sehingga tidak perlu di-redo
expect-result OK redone=[Tx2] undone=[Tx3]
expect acc 0:1 1 150
expect acc 0:2 2 250
expect acc 0:3 absent
expect-rows acc 2
